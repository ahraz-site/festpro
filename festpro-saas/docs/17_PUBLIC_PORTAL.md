# FestPro Public Portal — Complete Official Documentation

**Module:** Public Portal  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Dependencies:** Festival (04), Competition (05)  
**Applies To:** All website visitors (no login required)

---

# Section 1: Introduction

The Public Portal is the outward-facing website where potential participants discover festivals, view competition details, and register. It requires no authentication. Each organization gets a branded portal at `https://festpro.app/org/[slug]`.

---

# Section 3: Portals

| URL | Purpose | Example |
|-----|---------|---------|
| `/org/[slug]` | Organization public portal | `festpro.app/org/kerala-youth-festival` |
| `/org/[slug]/[festival]` | Specific festival | `festpro.app/org/kerala-youth-festival/2025` |
| `/register/[festival]` | Quick registration | Direct registration link |
| `/verify` | Certificate verification | Verify certificate by ID |

# Section 7: Step-by-Step Guide

**Step 1:** Visit the public portal link shared by the organizer.

**Step 2:** Browse the list of published festivals.

**Step 3:** Click on a festival to view details.

**Step 4:** Browse competitions under each category.

**Step 5:** Click **Register Now** to begin registration.

**Step 6:** Complete the registration form (no login required).

**Step 7:** Submit and pay (if fee applies).

**Step 8:** Receive confirmation with registration number.
