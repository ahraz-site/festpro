# FestPro Sponsorship & Exhibitor Management — Complete Official Documentation

**Module:** 36 — Sponsorship & Exhibitor Management  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies To:** Sponsorship Coordinators, Marketing Team

# Section 1: Introduction

The Sponsorship module manages sponsor relationships, exhibitor booth allocation, contract tracking, and sponsorship tier benefits. Supports the full lifecycle from lead tracking to post-event reporting.

## Key Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Sponsor Tiers | Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze with configurable benefits |
| Contract Management | Terms, amount, payment schedule, digital signing |
| Booth Allocation | Floor plan with booth assignment |
| Exhibitor Portal | Sponsor self-service portal for booth setup |
| Badge/Promo | Sponsor logo placement on public portal and materials |
| ROI Reports | Impression tracking, footfall, lead generation |

# Section 3: Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule |
|---------|------|
| SPON-001 | Sponsor logo appears on public portal based on tier (Platinum = hero, Gold = footer, etc.) |
| SPON-002 | Booth allocation is first-come, first-served within tier priority |
| SPON-003 | Contract payments must be received minimum 30 days before festival |
| SPON-004 | Sponsorship amount is recorded in finance module |
